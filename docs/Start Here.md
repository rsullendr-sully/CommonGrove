## What we are making

A warm, original 3D garden where multiple small, expressive spirits live, learn and build together. The pleasure is watching their personalities and capabilities develop—not managing workers or raising children.

Accomplishments quietly create opportunities: expanding the reading nook supplies knowledge; materials let spirits put that knowledge to use. Chao Garden is an experience reference, not a character design to copy. Preserve our approved warm, carved garden materials and compact cartoon creatures, with individual colors and features.

No hunger, sickness, decay, absence penalties or mandatory care. Workplace contributions must be treated equitably, without employee rankings or exposing private work details.

## Try the current learning-and-building loop

With the local development server running, open [the garden](http://127.0.0.1:3001/).

1. Open **Learning demo**, choose a scenario and select one or three residents.
2. Choose **Start**. An existing simulation requires replacement confirmation; Cancel keeps it. Only **Tool rack preview** seeds B1/B2 familiarity, with explicit Preview seed labels.
3. Use the offered book/material buttons. Materials-first needs the book; knowledge-first waits for real learning before materials; transfer unlocks rack materials after the planter completes.
4. Use **Watch** to turn toward the project, then walk normally toward the west reading nook. Leave room around the basket and work site. The knowledge panel distinguishes unfamiliar, familiar and practiced abilities and their sources.
5. Try **Replay** or return to a fresh ordinary journey when finished; both disclose that the current simulation will be replaced. Note observations below. Progress requires active simulation time.

The ordinary Garden journal and four-return journey remain available after leaving the demo. Keyboard Tab/Shift+Tab and Escape operate the demo controls.

The [abilities demo design](superpowers/specs/2026-09-08-spirit-abilities-demo-design.md) contains the catalog and staged acceptance criteria. ## How we keep this organized

- Start here for a dated overview and your observations.
- Keep designs, plans, decisions and research in their existing folders; link rather than duplicate them.
- Distinguish **proposed**, **approved**, **implemented**, **tested** and **owner accepted**. They are different milestones.
- The repository's root `PROJECT.md`, `STATUS.md`, `ROADMAP.md` and `BACKLOG.md` remain the canonical project records. They are one folder above this vault and can be opened in the project editor. This page is a dated guide, not a replacement for them.
- Notes are ordinary local Markdown files. No Obsidian account, sync service or community plugin has been configured here. Saving a note locally does not automatically commit or push it to GitHub.
