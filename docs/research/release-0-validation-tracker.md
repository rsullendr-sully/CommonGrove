## Suggested invitation

> We are testing an early, optional concept called Common Grove. It turns fictional examples of workplace accomplishments into changes in a calm virtual garden. We are looking for candid product feedback, not evaluating anyone's work. The session takes about 20–25 minutes, participation is voluntary, and individual responses will not be reported to management. The prototype is not connected to company systems. If you would like to help, please reply privately.

Do not send the invitation until the product owner chooses the facilitator and timing.
