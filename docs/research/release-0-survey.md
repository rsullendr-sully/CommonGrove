# Release 0 — Short Staff Survey

Use after an employee has explored the prototype. The survey should be anonymous where practical.

## Introduction

Common Grove is an optional product concept, not a performance-management system. This prototype uses fictional examples and is not connected to company systems. Please rate the experience candidly.

## Rating questions

Use a five-point scale: **Strongly disagree, Disagree, Neutral, Agree, Strongly agree**.

1. I understood why the garden changed.
2. The companion made the experience feel more personal.
3. I would be interested in returning to see what changed.
4. The experience felt rewarding.
5. The experience appeared to require very little attention from me.
6. People in my role would have fair opportunities to progress.
7. The garden felt separate from employee performance evaluation.
8. I understood that ordinary progress continues while choices are unanswered.
9. I would be comfortable with an opt-in coworker visit that reveals no underlying work information.
10. I am concerned that this concept could feel like workplace monitoring.

## Multiple-choice questions

### Which role group best describes your work?

- Support Desk
- Project Team
- vCIO / Strategic Advisor
- Service or Project Management
- Prefer not to say

### How often might you voluntarily open Common Grove?

- Several times per week
- About once per week
- A few times per month
- Less often
- Probably never

## Open questions

1. What felt most appealing?
2. What felt uncomfortable, confusing, or unnecessary?
3. What meaningful contribution from your role should the prototype recognize?
4. What information should always remain private?
5. What is the one change you would make first?

## Interpretation

Do not treat the survey as a vote or employee evaluation. Combine it with interview observations. Pay special attention to role-fairness responses, monitoring concerns, and whether stated return interest is voluntary rather than driven by workplace pressure.
