# Decision 0001 — Role-Equitable Progression

Status: Accepted  
Date: 2026-08-25

## Context

Common Grove serves employees whose work has different rhythms and artifacts. Support work may generate many short records, while projects, strategy, and management may produce fewer but broader milestones. Rewarding raw counts would create unequal progression and turn the garden into a disguised performance dashboard.

## Decision

Role-specific source events map into three universal contribution categories:

| Category | Support Desk | Project Team | vCIO / Strategic Advisor | Service and Project Managers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Helped someone succeed | Restored a user's ability to work; guided a user through a change; assisted another technician | Delivered a useful capability; helped a customer adopt a solution; assisted a teammate with a technical blocker | Helped a client make a sound decision; clarified priorities; guided a stakeholder through risk | Removed a delivery blocker; coached a teammate; coordinated help across teams |
| Moved important work forward | Resolved a meaningful issue; advanced a complex case; completed a planned follow-up | Reached a project milestone; completed a validated deployment; obtained customer acceptance | Delivered a roadmap or strategic review; completed an agreed initiative; turned a recommendation into an approved next step | Completed planning; brought a delivery back on track; closed an important operational commitment |
| Strengthened the team or system | Documented a reusable solution; identified a recurring problem; improved triage information | Created a reusable standard; improved deployment documentation; prevented a repeated implementation issue | Identified a systemic improvement; shared useful business context; created reusable strategic guidance | Improved a process; shared knowledge; improved workload visibility or delivery quality |

## Reward rules

- No essential resource, item, or companion path requires a role-exclusive activity.
- Universal categories influence story flavor, not access to progression.
- Ordinary events grant balanced reward bundles rather than a permanently fixed resource.
- Daily and rolling limits reduce advantages from high-volume systems.
- Larger milestones may change rarity or story significance without becoming mandatory.
- The system compares progression rates by role during testing and the pilot.
- Public rankings, grades, productivity scores, event counts, and reward totals are prohibited.
- The source system remains authoritative; the garden does not infer employee performance.

## Prototype event set

The first storyboard will select one example from each category for every role. Different source events will converge on the same three garden outcomes:

1. A dormant starflower blooms.
2. The companion advances a small reading nook.
3. A discovery seed unlocks a choice between an orchard and a workshop garden.

This deliberately demonstrates that different jobs can create comparable progress.

## Consequences

Integration mappings will require role-aware configuration and periodic fairness review. This extra work is accepted because equitable progression is central to trust and adoption.
